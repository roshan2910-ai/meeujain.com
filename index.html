A life in political journalism

1993 — PRESENT

More than three decades. One enduring vantage point: Raisina Hill.

Meetu Jain began her reporting journey in 1993. Since then, journalism has taken her through changing governments, political turning points and the stories that shape public life.

Raisina Hill has been more than a dateline. It has been a front-row vantage point on the institutions, decisions and people at the centre of India's public life. Over the years, nearly half of her life has been spent reporting from this political landscape — observing power, following questions and documenting the moments that matter.

This website brings that body of work together: a living archive of reporting across print, television and digital platforms, preserving the stories and the record behind them.
